# REQ-015 Decision Justification

REQ-015 was run through the agent after adding the tight-budget escalation rule. The resulting decision is `escalate`. Recorded rationale: "The escalate recommendation is driven by the budget check, vendor duplication check, policy compliance check, risk assessment check. The request for FastTrack Couriers totals $1,200.00, with policy context no policy ID. The evidence supports escalation because post-purchase remaining budget falls below 20% of the quarterly allocation and needs finance review. Low remaining budget after purchase: $4,300.00 (3.6% of quarterly budget), below 20% threshold."

This behavior is the right default for governance-first procurement because the request is legally and policy permissible, but the budget position is fragile and requires human oversight. Escalating avoids a false deny while still preventing automatic approval when discretionary capacity is near exhaustion, and it gives finance/procurement a chance to sequence spend, defer non-urgent purchases, or reallocate budget with accountability. The explicit low-remaining-budget rationale also improves auditability because the decision can be traced to a measurable threshold instead of implicit model judgment.
